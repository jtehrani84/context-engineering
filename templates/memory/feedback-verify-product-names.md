Verify every product name against the official documentation before it goes into customer-facing material. A plausible-sounding name that doesn't exist is worse than a vague phrase, because the customer will search for it.

**Why:** A draft follow-up email named a feature that had been renamed two releases earlier. The customer replied asking where to find it. The correction cost more credibility than leaving the name out would have. (Fictional example for this template.)

**How to apply:** When a draft names a product, feature or edition, look it up in the docs first. If you cannot confirm it, describe what it does in plain words and flag the name as unverified for me to check.

Source: my correction while reviewing a draft email.
